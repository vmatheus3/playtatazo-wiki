Your Tatazo

More guides

Looks, the boy or girl gator, and how the gear you wear shows on him.

You put on your first knight's helm. The blue crest disappears under the steel, the cape swings when you run, and your gator suddenly looks ready for anything. Every look you wear and every piece of gear you earn shows on your Tatazo.

Meet Tatazo

  • Tatazo is one hand-finished 3D gator. Every look is made on him, in the same style, so a hood fits his head and a cuirass fits his chest.
  • He is alive between your moves. He blinks, narrows his eyes when he taps his foot, pants with his mouth open on a run, and stretches into a big yawn when you leave him standing.

Looks

There are 78 looks to wear, each with its own colours and outfit: hoods, capes, knight's plate, robes, gis, beast armour and more.

  • Open the wardrobe: press E at the village wardrobe, or open the character menu (Tab) and turn to SKINS. Drag the preview to turn Tatazo round.
  • Every class has its own looks, and some looks are for everyone. Each card says whether you can wear it, what it costs in orange coins, or how to earn it.
  • Where looks come from: some are free, some cost orange coins (see Orange coins: earning and spending), and some are earned by playing. A few extra looks are sold in the store for real money; how that works, and how a grown-up controls it, is in Family safety features.
  • Looks are for fun only. They never make Tatazo stronger.

Boy or girl gator

Pick BOY or GIRL on the wardrobe page. The girl gator has eyelashes and a flower on her brow, and she wears every look. Other players see your choice.

Your gear shows

What you wear is what you see:

You wearTatazo shows
A helmetA leather cap on the first tiers, then a knight's helm. Class gear shows a mage's hood, a ninja's headband or a shooter's hat
Chest armourA cuirass, with shoulder plates once it is metal. Class gear shows a robe, a gi or bone plates
Leg armourPlates at the hips and greaves on the shins, or a robe's skirt or a ninja's wraps
BootsHis own shoes, in the boots' colours
An amuletA medal on his chest
A capeA cape down his back
A shieldOn his left arm, or on his back while he holds a bow
  • Gear covers your look where it sits. A helmet hides a hood or a hat; chest armour covers the look's own chest piece.
  • Colours follow the gear. Each piece shows its own colours, and the finest gear shines.
  • Your weapon rides on your back when you hold something else, and comes out when you fight.
Try it on first

The wardrobe's preview wears your gear too. Turn Tatazo round and see a look with your armour on before you choose it.

Where next

  • Earn better gear on The gear ladder, or buy and sell it on the market (The market and trading).
  • Take your look's picture in photo mode (P) and share its postcard code.
  • Try this tonight: stand still for a while in the village and watch Tatazo stretch and yawn.
