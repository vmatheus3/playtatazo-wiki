Map, minimap, pins and fast travel

More guides

Mark a far shore, find your way, and return to places you have earned.

The far shore is not lost

You find a bright temple beyond the trees, mark it, and carry on. Later, the same map brings you back to the place you discovered. Every trip makes the world feel more like yours.

Your map and minimap

Open the map with M. Drag to pan and zoom in or out to see a small path or a whole land. The map shows you, the village, your bed, other players, places you have found and your pins.

The minimap stays in the corner while you play. It is north-up and shows the nearby ground, creatures, players and discovered places. Its zoom controls show 100 m, 200 m, or 400 m across. Select the minimap to open the full map at that spot.

Find the people, and the boat

Looking for the ferry to the other islands, or for the one who sells rods? Open the map and zoom in on the village. Every person who lives there stands on the map exactly where they stand in the world, as a round gold badge in their own colour. A satchel means a shop, swords mean training, a book means tales and lessons, and a bubble means someone to chat with. Zoom in a little more to read their names, and closer still to read what they do. Hover a badge any time for the same words.

The ferry is the one you never lose. Its boat badge shows at every zoom, one at each continent's harbour, so you can spot where the next crossing starts from far away. Walk out along the pier to board.

Each town you have found shows its keeper too. Nana Purl and other hidden surprises stay hidden until you stumble on them yourself.

Not sure where to start? Press the FOLK button in the corner of the map. It jumps to the ferry first, and each press after that takes you to the next person, nearest to you first. It works with a controller too.

Pins and the compass

On the full map, choose an empty spot to make a pin. Choose a pin again to lift it. You can keep 12 pins, and they are saved with your character, so they wait for you next time too.

The compass shows your pins while you explore. Face one to see how far away it is. Use pins for a good fishing bend, a friend’s building site, or the trail back to a new landmark.

Travel to what you know

The village and your bed are travel points. Lookouts, temples, towns and certain special places become travel points after you discover them. Choose an unlocked travel point on the map and confirm the trip.

Travel has a level limit. You cannot jump to a place whose creatures start more than 10 levels above you. At level 30 you can travel to a level 40 area, but a level 50 area stays locked until you reach level 40. The lists show a locked place with a lock and the level to reach, and a refused trip costs nothing. Walking is always free, so a daring gator can still go the long way.

Travel is for a quiet moment, not an escape from danger. After taking a hit, wait 10 seconds before travelling. Anything Tatazo is riding stays behind, so dismount somewhere safe first.

Wayfinder Gem trips

The Wayfinder Gem is a separate, optional way to travel. Put it on your belt and press its 1–8 slot, then choose a friend in your world or a town. Friends accept every visit, and a town trip always ends beside its square. The gem can reach a town you have not found yet; when you arrive, ordinary map travel remembers it too.

When the gem works, a column of turquoise light with gold sparks rises where you leave and pours down where you land, and the edge of your screen glows for a moment. Friends standing nearby see the light too, so a visit never arrives as a surprise. After a successful arrival, the gem recharges for 30 minutes of real time. A refusal, a timeout, or stopping the charge costs nothing. It cannot travel during a fight, near a boss, in a friendly duel, in APOCALYPSE or in Survival. The same level limit applies: a town, or a friend standing in an area, more than 10 levels above you is too dangerous for your level yet. For a friend, the gem only says that; it never tells you where they are. The map's ordinary unlocked travel points remain free and have no Wayfinder recharge.

Pin a landmark before you wander away from it. Then explore freely, and use the compass instead of turning every journey into a careful retracing of steps.
