Diving and air

First steps

How to dive, how long your air lasts, and what waits at the bottom.

Swimming and diving

  • In water Tatazo floats with his head up and paddles. Use the move keys to swim.
  • Crouch (Ctrl, or B on a gamepad) to dive down.
  • Jump (Space, or A) to swim up.
  • Let go and the water gently lifts you back to the surface.

The first time you swim over deep water, the game shows you the keys.

Your air

With your head under water you have 20 seconds of air. Ten bubbles above your belt show how much is left. Air comes back four times as fast when your head is above water.

Out of air

When the bubbles run out you lose a tenth of your health every half second. Swim up! If you are knocked out under water, Tatazo floats up to the surface.

More air

  • Sea gear crafted from kelp, sea glass, coral and pearls adds up to 40 seconds more: look for Kelpweave Hood, Sea Glass Pendant, Reef Flippers and Coral Crown.
  • At the Glowstone Font, coral adds 4 seconds of air to a piece of headgear (see Item powers).
  • Ninjas learn Deep Dive at level 30: for 30 seconds they swim twice as fast and never run out of air.

What lives down there

Water deeper than about 2.5 m has its own creatures. They cruise near the bottom and follow divers up and down: see Deep water.

  • Gloom Piranhas come in schools.
  • Snapper Turtles sometimes carry a pearl.
  • Glow Jellies drift and spark.
  • The Deep Serpent is a fight for friends.

What you can gather

Under deep water grow kelp (in seas over 5 m deep), water weed, coral in warm seas and mussels in lakes and rivers (sometimes with a river pearl). You can find pearl beds, sea glass, shells, glowing anemones in the deepest places and, rarely, a sunken chest with finds, coins and sometimes gear for your level.

Before you dive

Eat something first, carry a potion, and know where the surface is. Diving is much easier with a friend watching your back.
