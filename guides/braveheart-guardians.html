Braveheart Basin: read the guardian, earn the opening

More guides

Learn the warning shapes, clear the helpers and make every safe opening count.

Aurendrake folds his wings. A bright line crosses the ground. You step aside, the dive passes, and the Old Warden's guarded heart opens. That is your chance: a calm head can take you further than a hurried attack.

Braveheart Basin's guardians are fierce protectors. You calm them by learning their movements. Each class has a way through each fight. Watch the warning, move to safety, then use the opening. There is no race against a hidden time limit.

How the waves grow

Let L be your level at the start of the wave and W its number. Normal creatures use min(130, L + floor((W − 1) / 20)). Guardians use your level, capped at 130. Your health and mistakes do not secretly raise the difficulty.

The normal creature budget is 4 + 2 × floor((L − 1) / 10), capped at 36. Arrivals come in 3 groups, 8 seconds apart. A guardian replaces the last arrival on its wave. Elite share rises in steps as your level rises; read the wave warning and choose which threat to calm first.

Keep something in reserve

The arena drains 2 fed seconds each real second. Eat before the meter empties: an unfed runner loses health and mana regeneration. Start with 3 mangoes, 1 health potion and 1 mana potion. Potions share a 0.9-second cooldown.

Normal creatures can give food (0.03 chance), health potions (0.02) or mana potions (0.02). Elites have a 0.1 chance of one supply. Each calmed guardian gives 1 chosen food and 1 potion. Use the 12-second wave break to gather and prepare; it does not heal you.

You have 3 lives. Before the last life is lost, a defeat returns you after 5 seconds with half health and mana and 5 seconds of protection. The creatures keep their progress. After repeated guardian defeats, choose when you are ready to try that guardian again.

Try this next: save a defence or movement skill for the guardian's warning instead of using every skill as soon as it is ready. Learn one clean dodge, then build the whole fight around it.
