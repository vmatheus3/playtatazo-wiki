Alpha 1.0.1

Tester update

2026-09-30 · The Wayfinder Gem, a new Play with Friends, walking with the mouse, and a privacy choice for game data.

What's new

  • Hold the left mouse button on open ground to walk forward, and steer with the mouse. You can turn it off in Settings, Controls.

  • Tester characters now start with a tamed Starmane Pegasus.

  • Choose whether to send anonymous game data from the new first-launch privacy screen, and change your choice later in Settings.

  • The Wayfinder Gem

    A gem of future-tech light that carries you to a friend or to any town in seconds. A friend always says yes first, a town trip ends beside its square, and after each trip the gem recharges for 30 minutes of real time.

    How to try it: Put the gem on your belt and press its slot, then choose To a Friend or To a Town. Alpha testers get it from the /tester command (type it in Quick chat and choose RUN); in the full game it is an optional store item.

Fixed

  • Scrolling lists, like crafting, now move about three rows per mouse-wheel notch, and trackpads scroll smoothly.

Changed

  • Play with Friends has the new look: one screen with Join, Host and Invites, and worlds shown as picture cards.

  • The Name Your Gator screen has the new look, with your gator on the pedestal.
