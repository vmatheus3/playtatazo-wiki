Menus and settings: find your way around

More guides

The title screen, mode cards, starting a world, pause menu and settings, with mouse, keyboard or controller.

You have been away all week. You open the game, press one button, and you are standing on the riverbank with the same gator, the same world and the lanterns just coming on. That is CONTINUE, and it is the first thing this guide wants you to find. Everything else on the menus is here to make the next trip easy too.

The title screen

Here is what you see, from top to bottom.

  • CONTINUE shows your last world, its mode, where you were and how long ago. Press it and you go straight in with your last gator. It only appears once you have a world.
  • PLAY is where you pick a way to play. It opens the mode cards.
  • PLAY WITH FRIENDS is where you join a friend's world or open yours. A small number shows when an invite is waiting.
  • SETTINGS, EXTRAS and QUIT sit in a smaller row. EXTRAS holds Credits and Send feedback for now. QUIT asks "Leave TATAZO?" first, and your worlds and gators are already saved.
  • The profile chip in the corner shows your gator's face. Press it to open YOUR GATORS: your gator cards (up to six), a new gator, edits, and the account and Steam status. Gators are managed here, and picked on the way into a world.

Going back never quits the game from the title.

The title, profile chip and gator cards use painted portraits. A gator's class supplies its portrait; a Rookie uses Tatazo's starting portrait. These pictures help you recognise the chosen gator. They do not change its class, equipment or owned looks. The painted backgrounds fit wide and tall screens without stretching the characters.

Pick a way to play

PLAY opens How do you want to play? It is a wall of picture cards. Each card has the mode's name, one line that sells it, three tags and a line under them: Your worlds: 2, or No worlds yet. A turquoise NEW badge sits on every mode you have never opened, until you open its page. On your very first visit, Adventure also wears a RECOMMENDED ribbon: it is the full game and the best place to start.

  • The first row is Adventure, Explore, Survival and Cozy. The second is Creative, Hardcore (its red 13+ gem means it is for teens) and the wide APOCALYPSE card, which shows New week when a fresh weekly board has started and you have not looked yet. On a child's profile the Hardcore card is not shown at all.
  • Press a card to open that mode's page. The guide button opens the mode's guide: F1 on a keyboard, Y on an Xbox-style pad, Triangle on a PlayStation one.
  • Not sure? Adventure is the full game. Read Game modes: pick your kind of world to compare all six.

A mode's page

Each mode's page has the same shape. The picture is on the left. On the right you read what it is, what you do in three lines and who it is best for. Underneath:

  • Your worlds in that mode: your three latest, newest first. Pick one to play it. All worlds on the same line opens the full list, where you can also rename or delete a world.
  • NEW WORLD to make another.
  • A Solo or Host for friends switch. Every mode can be played with friends; this is where you choose.
  • Read the guide, and BACK.

You never pick a class on these pages. Every gator chooses at Mira's shrine in the world.

Setting up a new world

  • World name. A friendly random one is filled in. Change it, or press the dice.
  • Seed. Leave it empty for a random world. Type a word to get the same world every time, or paste a postcard code a friend gave you to visit their kind of land.
  • Rules. Three tabs, BASICS, CREATURES and SURVIVAL (the Survival tab only appears in modes that use it). Each row has a help line. A small gold dot marks a row you changed from the mode's default, and a dimmed row is fixed by the mode. Reset to mode defaults puts everything back. The host can change most rules later in World settings.
  • CREATE, and then the last step: pick your gator and press START. Your last gator is ready, so it is one press. NEW GATOR is right there if you want a fresh one.

Name your gator

Choose NEW GATOR to name a new Rookie. Type a name or press RANDOM, then choose Boy or Girl and a free first look. The name must be 3 to 16 letters or numbers; spaces, hyphens and underscores are fine. The screen shows the cleaned name other players will see, or explains what needs fixing. Your new gator stands on the presentation pedestal while you choose. At level 8, every gator chooses one class at Mira’s Class Shrine, so a fresh gator is a good way to try another class. Press CREATE when you are ready, or LATER on your first gator to return to the picker.

In the world: the pause menu

Press Esc (the Menu button on a pad). In a solo world the game stops. In a shared world it keeps going, and the menu is called MENU.

  • RESUME goes back to the game.
  • SETTINGS opens the same tabs as the title.
  • HOW TO PLAY opens this world's page of How to play.
  • GO TO tiles jump to the map, crafting, building, the Skill Book, the journal, the players list, emotes and photo mode.
  • STUCK? RETURN TO SAFETY moves you somewhere safe if you are wedged in a spot.
  • FEEDBACK sends a bug report or an idea, with a picture of the game.
  • WORLD SETTINGS shows in your own world (the host's). It has the rule tabs for the world.
  • INVITE FRIENDS shows when you are hosting.
  • PLAYERS shows in a shared world, as a GO TO tile and a small button.
  • The small buttons share a row under the tiles. Hosting adds the world and invitation actions.
  • SAVE AND QUIT leaves. A host with friends in the world gets a check first: "Close the world?", because friends go back to their title. A guest sees LEAVE WORLD instead.

Settings, tab by tab

Open SETTINGS from the title or the pause menu. The tabs are GAME, GRAPHICS, AUDIO, CONTROLS, ACCESSIBILITY, FAMILY & PRIVACY and ACCOUNT. Changes take effect at once. The buttons that change tab are shown at the two ends of the tab bar. When a tab has more rows than fit, a thin gold bar on the right of the list shows where you are.

Game

  • Player names. Name plates over other players.
  • HUD size (Small, Normal, Large). Bars, the 1-8 bar and compass.
  • HUD width (Whole screen, Middle 21:9, Middle 16:9). On a very wide screen the bars, map and coins can stay near the middle, where your eyes are, instead of the far corners.
  • Tips and key hints. Reminders of the controls on the HUD.
  • Pickup notices. A faint line at the bottom right for each thing you pick up.
  • Minimap (Off, Near 100 m, Middle 200 m, Far 400 m). A small map in the bottom-left corner. Click it for the big map.
  • Pause when the window loses focus. Solo worlds only; shared worlds keep going.
  • Take-a-break reminder. A friendly note after this much play.
  • Never pick up. Marked items skip you. They still drop, and a party friend can still take them. Its list is right below. See Item pickup feed and Never pick up. These rows show inside a world.
  • Raccoon rows (if you own a raccoon helper): break down items, sell junk in town, and tidy both bags, each with its own list. See Meet Your Raccoon Helper.

Graphics

The Graphics preset (Auto, Very Low, Low, Medium, High, Ultra, or Custom) sets everything at once, and the rows below tune it: window mode, resolution, shadows, draw distance, plants round you, water, smooth edges, render scale, adaptive resolution, frame cap, show frame rate, soft corner shade, distant lands, wildlife and sparkles, people in the wild, glow, colour finish and upscaler. Every row is explained in Graphics settings.

Audio

  • Master volume. How loud everything is.
  • Music. The tunes that play as you go.
  • Effects and ambience. Footsteps, splashes, birds and the sounds of the world.

Controls

  • Mouse (Free pointer or Aim, locked). Free pointer: click things to act, and hold the right button to turn the camera. Aim: the pointer is locked and the crosshair picks what you act on.
  • Mouse sensitivity. How fast the camera turns.
  • Invert camera up and down. Push up to look down.
  • Field of view. How wide the world looks. A wider screen shows more at the sides by itself; a squarer one (4:3, 5:4, 16:10) opens the view a little so you lose less.
  • Auto-attack. Keep attacking the creature you click. Off: one attack a click.
  • Target lock. Keep the creature you click as your target.
  • Follow the target. Walk after the creature to reach it.
  • Reset keys, then the list of keys. Pick a key, press its new one, and Esc keeps the old one. Any free key works. If another action already holds it for good (walking, the number keys, Enter, Esc), the game says so and keeps your old key. A key you take from another action you can move gives that action your old key. Skills are on 1-8; the extra skill keys are optional.

More about the controls is in Controls, hotbar and tool wheel and Auto-attack, target lock and bite.

Accessibility

  • Reduce motion. Keeps the title background still instead of animating it.

Family & privacy

  • Family settings. Trades, spending, play time and reports for a grown-up. It asks for the PIN.
  • Streamer mode. Hides other players' names and your network addresses.
  • Let others see my gear. Other players can inspect what you wear.

Grown-ups can read what the game does for young players in Family safety features.

Account

  • Account and gators. Your account, your gators, their names and styles (from the title screen).
  • Send feedback. A bug report or an idea, with a picture of the game as it is.
  • Credits. A Duck Mines Game Studio game, with the fonts it uses named.

With a controller

Every menu works with a pad alone, and the hints at the bottom name the buttons of what you are using.

  • The d-pad or left stick moves the focus. A chooses and B goes one step back (Cross and Circle on a PlayStation pad; Enter and Esc on the keyboard).
  • On the Settings tabs and the world rule tabs, the tab buttons change tab: LB and RB on a pad (L1 and R1 on a PlayStation one), Q and E on the keyboard. Left and right change a value, and the hints show Change value while a row that can change has focus.
  • The extra action on a focused thing (R on the keyboard, Y or Triangle on a pad): it rolls the dice on the seed, resets a row, or resets to mode defaults.
  • The guide button (Y or Triangle) opens a mode's guide from its card or page.
Two minutes that pay off all week

Open SETTINGS > GAME once and set Take-a-break reminder and Minimap the way you like. Then open GRAPHICS and watch Show frame rate while you change a switch. After that, you can just press CONTINUE.

Where next

Compare the six in Game modes: pick your kind of world, learn the first steps in Getting started, or try the night that never ends in "Apocalypse: hold the line in Braveheart Basin".
