Hero's Health and Mana Potions

More guides

Two store potions that never run dry — a strong heal or a big mana refill, on their own cooldown, for good.

The potion that's always in your bag

You're deep in a fight, your last Great Health Potion is long gone, and the walk back to a shopkeeper is the last thing you want right now. That's exactly the moment Hero's Health Potion and Hero's Mana Potion are for: two flasks that never leave your satchel, no matter how many times you drink from them.

What they are

Hero's Health Potion and Hero's Mana Potion are separate one-time purchases through Steam's item store, at the price Steam shows in your currency. The moment you own one, a bottle appears in your satchel — unlike an ordinary potion in the game, it's never used up. Drink it, and it's still there, ready again once its cooldown runs out.

How it works

  • Hero's Health Potion heals 500 HP per drink.
  • Hero's Mana Potion restores 420 mana per drink.
  • Each has its own 20-second cooldown — longer than a normal potion's, so it's a convenience for the quiet moments between fights, not a way to out-heal a boss by mashing a key.
  • Drag it onto your hotbar like any potion and use that slot's shown action. It's still there afterwards.
Keep it on your bar, not in a drawer

Put your Hero's potions on the bar next to your regular ones. Save the regular stack for the thick of a fight, where the shared potion cooldown matters, and reach for your Hero's potion between fights or on the walk home. Check its cooldown before relying on the next drink.

What you can't do with it

  • It can't be traded, sold or dropped. It's bound to your account for good, the moment you buy it.
  • It doesn't work in a friendly duel or in APOCALYPSE — APOCALYPSE already gives everyone endless potions there, so bringing your own would just be unfair to whoever didn't buy one.
  • It affects play by restoring health or mana without spending a bottle. It is not cosmetic. Ordinary worlds, including Survival, allow it. The amount restored does not grow with your level.

Buying one

Open your character menu's SKINS page and switch to the STORE tab: Hero's Health Potion and Hero's Mana Potion sit alongside the Vault Key, the Building packs and the Travelling Pedlar. Real-money purchases are off until a grown-up turns them on with the PIN, and every purchase asks for the PIN. On a child's profile the store also stays hidden until a grown-up shows it. See Shop and paid items and Family safety features.

The item pages show the pictures: Hero's Health Potion and Hero's Mana Potion. Ordinary potions remain craftable or sold for orange coins; you do not need a paid bottle.
