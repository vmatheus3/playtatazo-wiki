Fishing

Life in the valley

Rods, bait, where and when each fish bites, and the legends of the river.

There is a fish that only stirs when its own realm is dark. Find the right water at the right time, feel the line pull, and bring home a catch with a story.

Casting

  • Craft a rod: a Bamboo Rod needs only bamboo or sticks and fibre.
  • Hold it and cast into open water.
  • Wait for a bite, then use the rod again to hook it. Hold use to reel, keep the line in the green, and ease off when the fish pulls.

Carry Fish Bait in your satchel and one is used each cast: bites come faster and rare fish come closer. Better rods cast further, and rare fish notice a shiny one.

Where and when

Each fish has its own waters, realm, time of day and sometimes weather:

  • Waters: rivers, lakes, the sea and wetland marshes.
  • Realm and night: some fish belong to one realm, and some only bite after dark there.
  • Time: dawn, day, dusk and night.
  • Weather: some fish bite more often in rain, fog, clear skies or storms, and a few bite only in one weather.
  • Depth: some big fish only live in deep water.

The table on Fish lists every fish with its hint. In the game, the almanac shows the same clue before you've caught one.

Junk and treasure

Now and then the line brings up an old boot, driftwood or pond weed. Driftwood snaps into sticks and pond weed makes compost, so even junk is useful. Rarely you'll pull up treasure: a bottle with a map, a River Pearl, a pouch of coins, a gold nugget, even moonstone. Better rods bring more treasure and less junk.

Trophies and legends

Rare fish and better are trophies: their length is kept with the fish. The legends of the valley are the ones everyone talks about:

  • Old Whiskers, a giant catfish of deep rivers on rainy nights,
  • the Golden Mango Koi, only at a clear dawn in calm lakes,
  • the Glyph Sturgeon, in the deepest water at night, its scales glowing like moonstone.

Cooking your catch

Common and uncommon fish grill into Grilled Fish at a campfire. A large catch can feed more than one meal. Grilled fish goes into fish stew and the fisher's rice bowl, which makes you a better angler for a while.

In a Survival world, raw fish spoils like other fresh food. Cook it, preserve it, or put it in an Icebox before it turns into compost. An icebox stops the freshness clock only for what is inside it.

Follow the clue

Open the almanac with a fishing tool or fish to check its clue. If a fish has not shown up, change the realm, weather or time instead of casting in the same spot all evening.

Where next

Take your catch to a campfire, then read Farming and cooking for meals and freshness, Survival worlds for Survival rules, and Getting around, and animal friends for reaching the next water.
