The Calm Aura

Life in the valley

Ten peaceful minutes for building and exploring, whenever you need them.

What it does

Press L (the right stick on a gamepad, or click the leaf badge in the row of buffs on your screen). For 10 minutes, creatures leave you alone:

  • no creature picks you as a target or chases you (any that were chasing turn back),
  • no creature's attack lands on you.

It's made for building, exploring, studying animals and taking photos in peace.

The rules

  • Striking a creature ends it. The aura is for peace, not for sneaking up.
  • It recharges for 60 minutes, counted from when you switched it on. The badge shows the time left, then the recharge.
  • The times follow the real clock, so logging out and back in neither resets the recharge nor loses the aura.
Great for
  • Building your first home far from the village.
  • Studying creatures for a storyline (Dr. Fernwhistle's steps say "a Calm Aura helps").
  • Walking past a strong creature to reach a place you want to see.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't protect you from lava crusts, running out of air or falling.
  • Other players in a world where fighting is on are not creatures: the aura doesn't stop them.
