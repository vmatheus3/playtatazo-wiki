The gear ladder

Fighting and gear

Crafted gear to level 35, dropped gear to 70, master-crafted gear from 75.

Weapons, armour, shields, capes, rings and amulets come in tiers, one every few levels. You can wear a piece once you reach its level. Where a piece comes from depends on its tier:

TiersWhere it comes from
1 to 35Basic gear: crafted at the workbench, anvil and loom. Creatures drop it now and then too.
40 to 70Dropped gear: no recipe. Only creatures drop it, and elites and mini-bosses drop more.
75 and upMaster gear: crafted again, from common metal plus rare master materials. Creatures drop it too, very rarely.

Every item's page says which kind it is, at the top of How to get it.

Some rare, epic and legendary pieces carry their own power too, like a sip of life back with every blow or quicker feet — and the Glowstone Font by the workshop can add one to a plain piece you already own. See Item powers: gear with a will of its own.

Basic gear: craft your next set early

A basic recipe needs you to be about five levels below the tier, so you can craft the next set while you're still growing into it. The materials climb with the tiers: flint and shells, then reeds and cloth, copper, iron, moonstone and, at the top, obsidian from the Ember Wastes.

Dropped gear: go and find it

From level 40, new gear only comes from cleansing creatures. A creature drops gear from the tier at or just below its own level (sometimes the tier above). Most finds will be for other classes. That's fine: trade them on the market board, and buy the pieces you need from others. Playing in a party helps too, because everyone near gets their own loot roll.

Master craft: rare materials

From level 75, the best gear is crafted again, at the anvil, the loom or the workbench. Each master recipe needs common materials (iron, gold, moonstone, cloth...) and two master materials. The strongest pieces also need a Guardian Core.

Creatures of level 70 and up drop their own realm's master material, but rarely: about 1 in 167 for an ordinary creature, far more often for elites and mini-bosses, and always for bosses. Guardian Cores come from elites (rarely), mini-bosses and bosses. If you can't reach a realm yet, look for its material on the market: other players may be selling it.

The world's loot rate changes these chances. Mini-bosses drop a master piece 2% of the time, bosses 10%. Heads, legs and feet take fewer materials than a full-size piece.

Crafting is the steady road. The lucky road is a drop: creatures of a high enough level drop master gear too, from about 800 cleanses for tier 75 to 10,000 for tier 200. Half of those finds fit your class. A find you can't use sells on the market, or breaks down into master materials before anyone wears it. Master gear has no fixed price: players set it. Read the odds and the bad-luck help in Lucky drops and rarity.
