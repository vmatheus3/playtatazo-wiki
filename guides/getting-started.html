Getting started

First steps

Your first hour in the valley, step by step.

The title screen

The title screen picks up right where you left off: CONTINUE shows your most recent world with a small picture of it as you left it, its realm and how long ago you played it, one press away. With no world yet, or to start another, PLAY opens your worlds and MULTIPLAYER joins or hosts one with friends. SETTINGS and QUIT sit below. Your profile, top right, is also where you switch to another one.

A looping picture of the world's places plays behind the menu, softened a little so the menu reads, and the corner names the place on show. If you would rather it held still, turn on Reduce motion in Settings > Video: it shows one calm picture instead of the moving one.

Welcome to the valley

You are Tatazo, a young gator who lives in a river valley. A strange grey murk called the Gloom has soaked into the land: it turns puddles into grumpy blobs and makes gentle animals cross. Your job is to explore, gather, build, and calm the creatures the Gloom has touched. Nothing is ever hurt for good. Gloom creatures burst into clean water and leaves, and animals turn friendly again and run home.

You start in the village. It is safe: no creatures come in, nobody can fight there, and nobody can dig or build in it. Walk out of the village and the adventure begins.

Your weapon, your tools and the 1–8 bar

  • Your weapon is the one you wear. Put a weapon in the Hand slot of your gear (the character menu, Tab). Every attack swings it: Tatazo draws it on his own, whatever he was holding. With no weapon worn, he bites.
  • The 1–8 bar holds your skills, food and potions. Nothing else goes there: no tools, no weapons, no building pieces.
  • A skill casts from its number key. Each new skill goes onto a free slot as you learn it, and in the spellbook (K) you drag skills onto the slots you like.
  • Food or a potion comes into your hand with its number; press the same number again to eat or drink one, just like a click. Put food there from your satchel (right-click it, or drag it).
  • Tools come out with the tool wheel. Press H and pick an axe, a pickaxe, a rod, a hoe… It stays in your hand until you pick another or draw your weapon (the wheel's top slot, or any attack). With a controller, hold RB, aim with a stick and let go. A tool you just crafted comes straight out, ready to use.
  • Seeds, building pieces and other things you hold: right-click them in your satchel (or pick them in the build menu) to take them out.
  • R, F, T and G are optional extra skill keys: each casts the skill you set for it in the spellbook. You can change them, like every key, in Settings > Controls. With a controller, the d-pad casts those four.
Playing with a controller

Everything works with a gamepad or a Steam Deck. The pause menu (the Menu button) has a GO TO list that opens the map, crafting, building, the spellbook, the journal, the players list, emotes and photo mode. Hints on screen name the buttons of whatever you are using.

Clicking works too

With a mouse, you can click things. Click a creature and Tatazo locks on and fights it. Click a tree, a rock or a plant and Tatazo walks over, takes the right tool out of his satchel and gathers it. Click the ground and he walks there. Hold the right button to turn the camera.

Three switches change how clicking fights, all on to begin with (in Settings > Controls, and as round buttons left of the 1–8 bar):

  • Auto-attack: Tatazo keeps attacking what he's locked on to. Off: one attack a click.
  • Target lock: a click locks on to a creature. Off: each click is one attack and nothing stays locked.
  • Follow the target: Tatazo walks after a creature that moves away. Off: he only attacks what is in reach.

Your first steps

The tracker on the screen always shows the next step. Here is the start of the rookie path:

  1. Meet Tiberius. He runs the village workshop. Walk up and press E.
  2. Pick up sticks outside the village (E on them).
  3. Gather plant fibre: punch bushes and tall plants (left mouse).
  4. Collect stones: pick up pebbles and flint, or break small rocks.
  5. Craft a stone axe: open crafting with C. Sticks, stone and fibre make your first Stone Axe.
  6. Chop down a tree: your new axe comes out in your hand (H takes it out later). Swing at the trunk until it falls.
  7. Craft a pickaxe: rocks and ore need one.
  8. Build a workbench: craft it and it comes out in your hand; place it. Many recipes need one: see Workbench.
  9. Cleanse the Gloom: calm three Gloom creatures outside the village, with the weapon in your Hand slot (or a bite).

After that come planting, fishing, cooking, building a home, turning a waterwheel, and at level 8 choosing your class. See the whole list on Quests and storylines.

Played before?

A new gator can skip the basic steps: on the character menu's HOW TO PLAY page, pick Skip them. You get the stone axe, the pickaxe, a workbench and the steps' coins (but not their XP), and the tracker moves on to the next step.

Health, mana, stamina and food

  • Health is your life. If it runs out you are knocked out (see Knocked out and getting up).
  • Mana powers spells. Stamina is for sprinting, dashing and some abilities.
  • Health and mana grow back while you are fed. Eat something: a mango, berries, or better, cooked food, which keeps you fed much longer. When you are starving, the edges of the screen pulse red.

Levels and XP

Everything you do gives experience (XP): gathering, crafting, cooking, fishing, calming creatures and finding new places. Levels have no cap. Each level takes a little longer than the last, but stronger creatures, on the lands further out, give more.

Every level makes you stronger: your hits and spells grow, and so do your health and mana. Past level 130, where the strongest creatures stop, each level adds even more (see Levels and power).

Achievements

On Steam, the things you do earn achievements. A world where the host has used the console to change things (give items, set levels, move about) is marked, and from then on it earns no achievements, the same rule as cheats in other games. Looking things up with the console doesn't mark it.

Photos and Pico's postcards

Photo mode (P) hides the screen's panels so you can take a picture, wide or tall for your phone. Every photo gets a postcard code from Pico the parrot. The character menu (J) lists them on its NOTES page, under Pico's Postcards, with a button to copy each one. Give a friend a code: in the same world it pins the spot on their map, and used as a new world's seed it grows that very world and pins the spot.

Day and night

A whole day lasts about 24 minutes. At night creatures are stronger and some only come out after dark. Sleep in a bed with E to make it your home (you get up there) and to skip the night when everyone in the world is asleep.

  • Make a friend: wild capybaras graze by the river (see Getting around, and animal friends).
  • Stuck? Ask on the forum. Someone has been there before.
  • Found a bug, or something that looks wrong? Press F7 (with a controller: Menu > Report a bug). The game takes a picture of the screen and asks what happened. The report stays on your computer.
