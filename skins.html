Skins

Looks

How Tatazo can look: free, earned by playing, and for orange coins.

Skins change how Tatazo looks, never what he can do. Every class has three free outfits, and more that its players can buy for orange coins earned in the game. Some looks are earned by playing, and new ones arrive now and then. Choose in the wardrobe (the SKINS page of the character menu). A girl-gator style can be worn with any skin.

The game also has a store of extra looks sold for real money, one at a time. This wiki does not list them. Real-money purchases stay off until a grown-up turns them on with the PIN, every purchase asks for the PIN, and on a child's profile the store only shows if a grown-up shows it: see Family safety features.
