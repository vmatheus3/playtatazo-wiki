Mosasaurus

Level 8+
Primeval IsleWater creature

Not a dinosaur: a giant sea lizard, cousin to today's monitor lizards and snakes. Read the tale

Field notes

Not a dinosaur: a giant sea lizard, related to today's monitor lizards and snakes. It ruled the Late Cretaceous seas, 82-66 million years ago.

Where it lives

Primeval Isle, in Primeval. Seen from level 8. Its level depends on where you meet it: at home, how far from the village you are; on the other lands, that land's band, lowest near the harbour side. It is stronger at night.

How to calm it

Weak to (takes more): Energy +40%Earth +20%

Its attack: the sparks. It is steady (wind-up 0.8 s, then 2.4 s before the next).

How to dodge

Mosasauruses are weak to energy and earth. Watch for their sparks: step sideways when they glow, and the sparks fly past. Cleansed, they turn back into friendly animals.

Cleansed, it turns back into a friendly animal and wanders home.
