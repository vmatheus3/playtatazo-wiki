Snow Burrower

Level 7+
FrostlandsBrute

If a snowdrift blinks at you, walk the other way.

Field notes

Waits under the snow with only its eyes showing, then bursts out in a spray of powder.

Where it lives

Frostlands, in Tundra, Taiga, Glacier. Seen from level 7. Its level depends on where you meet it: at home, how far from the village you are; on the other lands, that land's band, lowest near the harbour side. It is stronger at night. Stronger ones are called Murky Snow Burrower (level 15+), Deep Snow Burrower (level 40+), Elder Snow Burrower (level 80+).

How to calm it

Weak to (takes more): Fire +30%Energy +10%

Tough against (takes less): Ice −40%

Its attack: the leap. It is steady (wind-up 0.85 s, then 3.1 s before the next).

How to dodge

Snow Burrowers are weak to fire and energy but shrug off ice. Watch for their leap: step out of the ring that shows where they'll land.
