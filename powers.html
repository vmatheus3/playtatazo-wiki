How powers work

Some gear does more than protect you: boots that make you run faster, rings that bring back health or mana, blades that draw health from the creatures you fight. A power is part of the item (every copy has it) or imbued into one piece at the Glowstone Font. Everything you wear adds up, with limits so no outfit breaks the game: speed up to 35%, drawing health or mana up to 15% each, critical chance from gear up to 25%, extra air up to 40 seconds.

The Glowstone Font

A glowing basin beside the village workshop. Bring a realm's essence and a few coins, and one piece of gear takes one step of a power: at most 3 steps a piece. It costs 24 coins at level 1, 60 coins at level 10, 100 coins at level 20, 140 coins at level 30, 220 coins at level 50, 420 coins at level 100.
