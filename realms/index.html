Realms

The world is an ocean with eight lands in it, and each land is one realm. The Riverlands are home, round the village. The others lie across the sea, each with its own ground, weather, music, creatures and treasures, and its own band of creature levels (see Continents and danger). Deep water, in every realm, has creatures of its own.
