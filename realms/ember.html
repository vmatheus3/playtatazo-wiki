Ember Wastes

Levels 90–120

Ash plains, basalt and smoking volcano cones. The glowing lava crusts burn: keep off them.

What it is like

WhereA continent about 1.6 km across, across the sea; its middle is about 3.9 km from the village.
DangerCreature levels 90–120, climbing from the harbour side (the coast facing the village) to the far side. More at night, on glacier peaks and in volcano craters.
TownsSafe towns with stations and a trading post kept by Cinda the Salamander
Looks likeGrey ash plains, black basalt and obsidian spires, smoking volcano cones with glowing craters, charred trees with embers still in them. Life comes back after the fire: groves of silvery ash pines, fire palms with red-orange fronds and orange coconuts, lava coral trees that branch like coral, and small obsidian thorn trees with glossy black bark and red leaves. Old charred stumps have bright green saplings growing out of them. Fireflower shrubs with orange trumpet flowers, ember ferns with red-tipped fronds, mounds of yellow sulphur moss on black stone and patches of dark cinders with little red flowers dot the ash. Warm spring pools steam gently in rings of orange and turquoise rock, red basalt columns glow in their cracks, and pale pumice rocks lie about. Now and then a sleeping volcano stands on the plains, a landmark you can see from far away, with a wisp of steam over its crater. Fell the trees with an axe for logs (the fire palm's coconuts come with E, the thorn tree gives thorns, and a stump sprout gives charcoal and now and then a sapling to plant). Cut fireflowers for red petals and blossoms, pick ferns for fibre and cinder patches for red petals, pull moss off the stones for fibre and pebbles, and break basalt columns (a copper pickaxe) and pumice for stone and gravel. The warm springs and the sleeping volcano are left as they are.
WeatherFalling ash and drifting embers in an orange haze.
MusicBarrel drums and horns over a deep lava rumble.
GroundWater turns into a glowing lava crust here. It burns.
Region namesEmberdeep Caldera, Cinder Wastes, Ashfall Barrens, Scorch Ashfields…

Watch out

  • Lava crusts burn: standing on one costs 6% of your health every 0.5 s as fire. Fire resistance helps; better, keep off.
  • In volcano craters creatures are 2 levels (and a tenth) stronger.
  • Ash Drifters drift out after dark.
  • The Caldera Drake nests in the craters.
  • Magmaw the Caldera King, the oldest drake, bursts from the ash on the far side of the Wastes.

Tips

  • Ember vents give ember cores, the heart of fire gear.
  • A stump sprout is new life after a fire: its sapling, planted at home, grows into a blossom tree.
  • Obsidian and hound fangs make Cinderstride Boots, which also resist fire.
