Deep water

Every realm

Lakes, rivers and seas more than 2.5 m deep, in every realm where the water is not frozen or molten.

What it is like

Crouch to dive and jump to swim up. You have 20 seconds of air; watch the bubbles above your belt. See Diving and air.

WhereUnder the surface of deep water, near the bottom.
Looks likeKelp forests in seas over 5 m deep, water weed, coral in warm seas, mussels, pearl beds, sea glass, glowing anemones and, rarely, a sunken chest.
WeatherQuiet and blue.
MusicWhatever realm you are diving in.

Watch out

  • Out of air, you lose a tenth of your health every half second: swim up!
  • Gloom Piranhas come in schools.
  • The Deep Serpent is a fight for friends.
