Glowcap Hollow

Levels 25–50

Violet moss and mushrooms taller than houses; at dusk the whole hollow glows.

What it is like

WhereA continent about 1.2 km across, across the sea; its middle is about 2.2 km from the village.
DangerCreature levels 25–50, climbing from the harbour side (the coast facing the village) to the far side. More at night, on glacier peaks and in volcano craters.
TownsSafe towns with stations and a trading post kept by Morel the Toad
Looks likePurple moss and woods of mushrooms taller than houses. Giant glowcaps with wide blue caps and big pale spots fill the woods, with stands of tall violet parasol mushrooms in some places and lantern stalks, brown trunks hung with glowing lantern pods under orange and teal caps, in others. Twin shelf fungus trees, two trunks twisted together and stepped with orange shelves, grow among them, and soft moonpuffs as big as a house, cream with pastel blushes, stand where the woods thin out. Under the trees grow glowing ferns with turquoise tips, clusters of round spore bulbs and little glowcaps; the woods open into clearings with carpets of tiny colourful mushrooms, mounds of moss with pink crystals, and small puddles full of glowing blue algae. Pale stone knobs with little mushrooms on top are the common rocks, and past the first stretch of the hollow you can find geode rocks split open on a cave of purple crystals. Far out, now and then, two giant toadstools lean together into a great arch: a landmark you can walk under. After dusk the spots, lantern pods, fern tips, spore bulbs, crystals and puddles all glow. Fell the giant mushrooms with an axe for mushroom fibre, caps to eat and glow spores; lantern stalks and twin shelf trees also give logs. Pick spore bulbs for glow spores, mushroom carpets for mushrooms, and scoop pond weed out of a glow puddle; cut glowing ferns and crystal moss for fibre. Break pale stone knobs with a pickaxe for stone and pebbles (and sometimes a mushroom), and geodes, with a stone pickaxe or better, for stone, flint and pebbles.
WeatherDrifting glowing spores in a violet dusk.
MusicA music box, a glassy hum and dripping water.
Region namesGlowcap Hollow, Moonshroom Vale, Sporewood Glen, Duskspore Grove…

Watch out

  • Glow Moths throw sparks from a distance.
  • The Toadstool Brute leaps and leaves a fairy ring where it lands.
  • Mother Morel, every sporeling's grandmother, lives in the deepest hollows.
  • Queen Morel rises from the moss on the far side of the hollow.

Tips

  • Mushrooms are fungi, not plants: they help rot old wood back into soil.
  • Glow spores and mushroom fibre make the Sporelight Ring for Mages.
  • The great toadstool arch can't be cut down: look for its red caps far out in the hollow's clearings to find your way.
  • Geode rocks need a stone pickaxe or better: a wooden one only scratches them.
