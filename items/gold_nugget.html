Overview

How to get it

  • Creatures that drop it

    When a creature is cleansed. Elites drop it half as often again. The world's loot rate changes these chances.

    CreatureRealmChanceHow manyFrom level
    Gold DragonDragonfire Isle100%4–836
    GoliathRiverlands100%3–6999
    The Great Sun GuardianSunscorch Desert100%3–6999
    Sun GuardianSunscorch Desert80%2–414

  • Gather it

    FromHowChanceHow manyWhere
    Iron Veinmine it (pickaxe, tier 3 or better)8%1Riverlands
    Sunstone Veinmine it (pickaxe, tier 3 or better)10%1Sunscorch Desert
    Sunken Chestpick it up (E)60%1–2Deep water

  • Catch it

    Sometimes a line brings this up instead of a fish (treasure). Better rods and luck bring more treasure and less junk.
