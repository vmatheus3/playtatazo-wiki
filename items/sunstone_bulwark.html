Sunstone Bulwark

Uncommon

Level 60 · Knight · Armour · Off hand

Overview

A knight's shield. Blocks blows from the front.

How to get it

  • Dropped gear Dropped by creatures of about level 56 and up; elites and mini-bosses drop more. Not crafted. The gear ladder

  • Find it

    Creatures from level 51 up can drop it now and then as gear loot.

Crafted in Explore worlds

Explore worlds Crafted in Explore worlds at the anvil from Sunscorch Desert materials. Creatures drop it too.
