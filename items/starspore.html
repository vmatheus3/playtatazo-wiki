Starspore

Rare

Material

Overview

A spore that shines like a tiny star. It floats a finger's width above your palm.

How to get it

  • Master material Master material. Level 70+ creatures of Glowcap Hollow drop it, about 1 in 167; elites, mini-bosses and bosses more often. Others may sell it on the market. The gear ladder

  • Creatures that drop it

    Creatures of level 70 and up from Glowcap Hollow drop it when they are cleansed: about 1 in 167; elites about 1 in 33; mini-bosses about 1 in 3; bosses every time. The world's loot rate changes these chances.
