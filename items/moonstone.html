Moonstone

Rare

Material

Overview

A glowing turquoise gem from deep caves. The old builders' power.

How to get it

  • Creatures that drop it

    When a creature is cleansed. Elites drop it half as often again. The world's loot rate changes these chances.

    CreatureRealmChanceHow manyFrom level
    GoliathRiverlands100%2–4999
    Queen MorelGlowcap Hollow80%2–4999
    Deep SerpentDeep water50%1–212
    Moss GolemRiverlands7%112
    Mycelium GolemGlowcap Hollow6%112

  • Gather it

    FromHowChanceHow manyWhere
    Moonstone Clustermine it (pickaxe, tier 4 or better)100%1–2Riverlands
    Sunken Chestpick it up (E)10%1Deep water

  • Catch it

    Sometimes a line brings this up instead of a fish (treasure). Better rods and luck bring more treasure and less junk.
