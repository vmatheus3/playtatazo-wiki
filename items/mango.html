Food

Overview

Tatazo's favourite. Heals a little and keeps you fed.

How to get it

  • Gather it

    FromHowChanceHow manyWhere
    Mango Treeharvest it (E), grows back100%2–3Riverlands

  • Grow it

    • Mango Tree: 3–5 per harvest. Plant Mango Sapling; ready in 4 game days, then again and again.

    Farming guide

  • Buy it

    • Mira the Capybara sells it for 4 coins (up to 10 a day).
    • Otto the Otter sells it in every Riverlands town for 4 coins (up to 10 a day).

  • Starter kit

    Every new gator starts with 5.
