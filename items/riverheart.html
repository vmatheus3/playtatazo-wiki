Riverheart

Legendary

Level 100 · Knight · Weapon · Hand

Overview

The legendary blade of the river's guardians. It glows turquoise, and gloom melts before it.

How to get it

  • Named find A named find: creatures drop it rarely, bosses and mini-bosses more often. Not crafted. The gear ladder

  • Find it

    A Legendary find: any creature from about level 76 up can drop it as rare gear. Mini-bosses and bosses always roll Rare or better, and elites roll better than most.

Crafted in Explore worlds

Explore worlds A named piece: in Explore worlds it's crafted at the anvil from Riverlands materials and its token. Creatures drop it rarely.
