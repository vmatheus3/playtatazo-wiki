Overview

A handful of sweet berries.

    When a creature is cleansed. Elites drop it half as often again. The world's loot rate changes these chances.

  • Buy it

    • Mira the Capybara sells it for 2 coins (up to 10 a day).
    • Otto the Otter sells it in every Riverlands town for 2 coins (up to 10 a day).
    • Hazel the Hedgehog sells it in every Autumn Woods town for 2 coins (up to 10 a day).
    • Nils the Snow Hare sells it in every Frostlands town for 2 coins (up to 10 a day).
    • Zahra the Fennec Fox sells it in every Sunscorch Desert town for 2 coins (up to 10 a day).
    • Cinda the Salamander sells it in every Ember Wastes town for 2 coins (up to 10 a day).
    • Morel the Toad sells it in every Glowcap Hollow town for 2 coins (up to 10 a day).
    • Iggy the Iguana sells it in every Primeval Isle town for 2 coins (up to 10 a day).
    • Bram the Badger sells it in every Dragonfire Isle town for 2 coins (up to 10 a day).
