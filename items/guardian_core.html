Guardian Core

Epic

Material

Overview

The calm, glowing heart a great guardian leaves behind when its gloom is cleansed. Master smiths build their best work round one.

How to get it

  • Master material Master material. Mini-bosses of level 70+ drop it about 1 in 4, bosses every time. Others may sell it on the market. The gear ladder

  • Creatures that drop it

    Creatures of level 70 and up drop it when they are cleansed: elites about 1 in 100; mini-bosses about 1 in 4; bosses every time. The world's loot rate changes these chances.
